1.1 Operator Notation

NoteDefinition

A differential operator \(L\) is linear iff for any two functions \(y_1\) and \(y_2\)

\[L[c_1y_1+c_2y_2]=c_1L[y_1]+c_2L[y_2]\]

1.2 Example 1

TipExample

Let \[ L = 3\frac{d}{dx}+2\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\]

CautionSolution

\[L[c_1y_1+c_2y_2]=\left(3\frac{d}{dx}+2\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\right)\left(c_1y_1+c_2y_2\right)\] \[=c_1\left(3\frac{d}{dx}+2\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\right) y_1+c_2\left(3\frac{d}{dx}+2\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\right) y_2\] \[=c_1L[y_1]+c_2L[y_2]\]

1.3 Theorem for higher order

NoteTheorem

Theorem 1.1 If \(a_n(x),a_{n-1}(x),\cdots,a_0(x)\) are continuous over the interval \(I\), and if \(a_n(x)\neq 0\) then \[L[y]=0 \tag{1.1}\] where \[L=a_n(x)\frac{d^n}{dx^n}+\cdots+a_1(x)\frac{d}{dx}+a_0(x)\] has \(n\) linearly independent solutions \[y_1(x),\cdots,y_n(x)\] Furthermore if \(y(x)\) is any solution to Equation 1.1 then \(\exists \, c_1,c_2,\cdots,c_n\in\mathbb{R}\) such that \[y(x)=c_1y_1+c_2y_2+\cdots +c_ny_n\]

1.4 Wronskian

NoteDefinition

The Wronskian of a set of functions \[f_1(x),f_2(x),\cdots,f_n(x)\] is equal to the determinant

\[\begin{vmatrix} f_1(x) & f_2(x) &\dots & f_n(x)\\ f_1'(x) & \ddots && \vdots\\ \vdots &&\ddots&\vdots\\ f_1^{(n-1)}(x) & f_2^{(n-1)}(x) &\dots & f_n^{(n-1)}(x) \end{vmatrix}\]

and is denoted by \(W[f_1(x),\cdots,f_n(x)]\)

1.5 Theorem for Wronskian

NoteTheorem

Theorem 1.2 \(\{f_1(x),f_2(x),\cdots,f_n(x)\}\) are linearly independent on \(I\) iff \[W[f_1(x),\cdots,f_n(x)]\neq 0\]

1.6 Example 2

TipExample

\(f_1(x)=\cos x,\qquad f_2(x)=\sin x\)

1.7 Example 3

TipExample

\(f_1(x)=e^x,\qquad f_2(x)=x e^x\)

1.8 STHOLH/ODES/WCC

NoteDefinition

Solutions to higher order Linear Homogeneous ODES1 with constant coefficients

\[a_{n}y^{(n)}+a_{n-1}y^{(n-1)}+\cdots+a_1y'+a_0y=0 \tag{1.2}\]

Characteristic Equation:

\[a_nr^n+a_{n-1}r^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1r+a_0=0 \tag{1.3}\]

has \(n\) solutions.

1.9 Distinct real roots

If \(r_1,\cdots,r_k\), with \(k\leq n\), are distinct real roots of Equation 1.3 then

\[y=c_1e^{r_1x}+c_2e^{r_2x}+\cdots+c_ke^{r_kx}\] is a solution to Equation 1.2

1.10 Complex real roots

If \(\alpha_1\pm i\beta_1,\quad\alpha_2\pm i\beta_2,\quad\cdots\quad,\alpha_k\pm i\beta_k\), are roots of Equation 1.3 with \(k\leq \frac{n}{2}\). Then \[y=e^{\alpha_1x}(c_{11}\cos\beta_1x+c_{12}\sin\beta_1x)\] \[+e^{\alpha_2x}(c_{21}\cos\beta_2x+c_{22}\sin\beta_2x)\] \[+\cdots+e^{\alpha_kx}(c_{k1}\cos\beta_kx+c_{k2}\sin\beta_kx)\] is a solution to Equation 1.2

1.11 Repeated real roots

If \(r=m\) is a \(k\) repeated root of Equation 1.2, with \(k\leq n\), Then

\[y=c_1e^{mx}+c_2xe^{mx}+c_3x^2e^{mx}\cdots+c_kx^{k-1}e^{mx}\] is a solution to Equation 1.3

1.12 Example 4

TipExample

Solve:

\[y^{(5)}+3y^{(3)}-4y'=0\]

CautionSolution

Characteristic eq\(^n\):

\[r^5+3r^3-4r=0\] \[r(r^4+3r^2-4)=0\]

Let \(u=r^2\) \(\rightarrow (r^4+3r^2-4)=(u^2+3u-4)=(u-1)(u+4)\)

\[\Rightarrow r(r^2-1)(r^2+4)=0\]

\[r(r+1)(r-1)(r+2i)(r-2i)=0\]

\[r_{oots}=0,-1,1,-2i,2i,\] \[\boxed{y=c_1+c_2e^{-x}+c_3e^x+c_4\cos 2x+c_5\sin 2x}\] is the general solution.
